Agent Video: POST /agent/video

Generate an 8-second 720p video clip with native audio from a text prompt. One call costs $4.99 in USDC on Base.

What it is for

Short-form video, b-roll, product shots, music-video visuals, social clips and ads. The audio is generated from the scene, so write the sound into the prompt: instruments, voices, weather, movement. A silent scene renders near-silent by design.

Request body

FieldTypeRequiredDescription

Example: a vertical social clip

Get the 402 challenge first (amount 4990000, priceUsd $4.99), sign it as shown in the quickstart, then send:

curl -i -X POST https://app.suedeai.ai/agent/video \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -H "PAYMENT-SIGNATURE: $PAYMENT" \
  -H 'X-Idempotency-Key: vinyl-clip-0001' \
  -d '{
    "prompt": "Slow push-in on a spinning vinyl record, rain against the window, soft piano and the crackle of the needle",
    "aspectRatio": "9:16"
  }'

The prompt names its sounds (rain, piano, needle crackle) because the audio track is generated from what the scene describes.

Response to a paid call: 202 Accepted

The discovery document gives this example body:

{
  "jobId": "video-job-example",
  "status": "queued",
  "provider": "suede",
  "pollUrl": "https://app.suedeai.ai/agent/video/video-job-example"
}

With ?async=false the call blocks and answers 200 with the same body shape as the poll below, so one parser covers both paths. Renders take minutes and payment clients time out in about 30 seconds, so most callers should keep the default and poll.

Polling: GET /agent/video/{jobId}

Poll until status is completed, with videoUrl set, or failed. Both are final; queued and processing mean poll again.

FieldDescription
jobIdThe render's job identifier.
statusqueued, processing, completed or failed.
providerThe provider handling the render.
videoUrlThe generated video once complete; null until then.
videoIdThe Suede video record identifier, when available.
pageUrlThe Suede share page, when available.
pollUrlSet on the 202 accept; null on a poll response.

An unknown or expired jobId answers 200 with status failed, not 404. On 2026-10-11 that response also carried "error": "Generation failed. Please try again." and an etaSeconds field set to null.
